# Flux privacy policy draft

Effective date: November 14, 2026.

Flux creates wallpaper images and animations on your device. It does not create an account, contact an app-operated server, include advertising, or collect analytics.

Your wallpaper style, favorites, colors, motion preferences, and haptic setting are stored in the app's local preferences. Use Settings → Reset preferences in Flux to restore those preferences to their defaults.

When you choose Save Live Photo, Flux asks iOS for permission to add a photo and its paired video to your photo library. It does not request access to browse or read your existing photos. Saved items remain in Photos until you remove them. If your device uses iCloud Photos, Apple may sync those saved items according to your Apple settings.

When you share an image or video, iOS presents the system share sheet. The destination you choose handles the shared file under its own policies.

Temporary export files are removed after a completed save or after dismissing the share sheet. iOS may retain temporary files if the app is terminated during export.

When distributed through TestFlight, Apple may handle diagnostics and tester feedback under Apple's policies and your TestFlight settings. The Flux source code itself contains no analytics SDK.

Before publishing this policy, add the app operator's real contact information and verify the behavior of the final signed build.
